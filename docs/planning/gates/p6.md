Any unmet P6.1-P6.6 result or a `no-go` decision blocks F1, including failures in workflow,
measurement, data integrity, authorization, blind disclosure, accessibility, responsive use,
performance, recovery, or operations. A failed gate returns work to the owning P sprint and
requires rerun evidence.

Any change to the participant-facing instrument build after P6.3's rehearsal has run, including a
change to an already-merged R7b, voids P6.3: the rehearsal must be repeated against the changed
build before F1 begins. This holds regardless of whether the change lands before or after P6 has
otherwise closed. The rule exists because F1's initial four evaluators cannot redo their one blind
pass through the instrument once it starts, so a rehearsal that validated a build F1 will not
run proves nothing about the instrument that matters. The evaluator property guide
(`facilitated-property-guide`) is a reading aid outside that rule: a later wording change to it does not void P6.3,
provided it adds no recorded field and the F1 declaration names the version id used.

## Facilitated-format scope for P6.2 and P6.3 (added 2026-11-25)

F1 may run as the six-evaluator facilitated format decided on 2026-11-24 (see the [F1 gate](f1.md)). In that case
P6.2 and P6.3 must cover it; a rehearsal of only the one-evaluator, on-screen path does not rehearse the instrument
F1 will use. This section is a scope requirement, not evidence: nothing here is recorded as done.

**P6.2 additions.** The device matrix covers the single recorder login that transcribes all six evaluators' paper
sheets (one recorder session, one evaluator at a time, on the facilitator's device), and the manager actions the
format needs: session-plan preview, the non-blind call, program checkpoints, the readiness view and the household
reveal.

**P6.3 additions.** The synthetic rehearsal must run the paper plus transcription flow in the six-person format:

- six synthetic evaluators, shared triples with per-session Latin-square rotation and random per-evaluator codes (`INDIVIDUAL` blotter mode), printed
  [session sheets](../../facilitated-session-sheet.md) and [conditions slips](../../facilitated-conditions-slip.md)
  filled in on paper, and the facilitator transcribing every sheet through one recorder login;
- the holder flagged non-blind, a checkpoint created for every enrollment before any holdout is touched, a
  household reveal after the holdouts, and the facilitator spray-sheet generator exercised on a synthetic mapping
  kept outside the repository;
- confirmation of the **provisional 2-minute spray-to-first-smell ceiling** (owner decision 2026-11-25, replacing the
  earlier 5 minutes and kept at 2 minutes after the group blotter attempt): measured per evaluator's own blotter,
  counted from that evaluator's own spray to that evaluator's own first smell, with the actual sprayed, first-smelled
  and column-finished times and any breach recorded as a deviation. The rehearsal must test whether all six
  evaluators, each spraying their own blotter, can keep inside 2 minutes, **including at least one fresh blotter** (a
  new blotter of the same sample, same blind code, sprayed when that evaluator's window passed unsmelled, with the
  sheet's Time sprayed being the spray time of the blotter that evaluator smelled), and must record **how often a
  fresh blotter was needed**. The 2 minutes is a working value, not a researched one; the rehearsal confirms it or
  replaces it, and a changed value is recorded in the F1 pre-exposure declaration. The 2 minute clean-air interval
  between samples (ADR-005) is a different number and is checked separately;
- confirmation of the **individual blotter procedure and roles**: one blotter per evaluator per sample, each evaluator
  spraying their own (spray distance and count are an instruction to each evaluator; the owner decided on 2026-11-25
  one spray from about 15 cm, pointed away from faces and other blotters), the facilitator
  organizing blotters, trays and sheets and enforcing silence and clean air **without evaluating** in those sessions,
  evaluators walking up to the atomizers as they are ready to spray their own blotter, with no vial number or identity
  exposed (the atomizer label printed under each blind code on the header and on the container is the only location
  key shown; decided 2026-11-27, sheet `v2.2`) and the facilitator not scheduling or controlling turns at the vials (owner decision 2026-11-26, which changes
  no recorded field, the sheet or the slip, so it does not void this rehearsal; the dry run records how often
  evaluators queued or crowded at the vials and how many replacement blotters were needed, and the facilitator notes in
  the rehearsal log if the walk-up approach had to be changed, which would be a new owner decision), and
  paper plus transcription of the resulting sheets (each evaluator's own Time sprayed entered). The holder's own
  evaluations are rehearsed separately, single-blind with observer cues. The rehearsal confirms the procedure or
  records a different one. The group blotter procedure was tried by the owner on 2026-11-25 and abandoned (two
  evaluators in two minutes), so it is not rehearsed;
- the **transcription and holdout safeguards deliberately attacked**, each recorded in the rehearsal log: (1) enter one
  evaluator's sheet under the wrong evaluator and record whether anything catches it (with individual blotters every
  evaluator's codes are their own, so the blind-code guard is expected to refuse it with a 409 when the code is typed
  from the paper; confirm that it does, and record it honestly if it does not); (2) withdraw one of the six
  evaluators and record what happens to the household reveal (no withdrawal path exists, so this documents the gap
  rather than passing it); (3) a holdout column whose times pre-date the checkpoint, which the server must refuse with
  a 422; and (4) a holdout write for an evaluator who has no checkpoint of their own, which the server must refuse
  with a 409 (the gate is per enrollment in an individual program). These do not exercise the facilitator's own rule of
  not smelling a holdout before all six checkpoints exist, which is procedure and is observed, not tested;
- confirmation of the **paper-retention period**: the owner decided on 2026-11-25 that sheets, slips and scans are kept
  12 months after the evaluation ends and destroyed together with the database and its backups (the consent text's
  period). The rehearsal shows that scans can be stored and later destroyed together with the database and its
  backups.

**The merged code is the rehearsed instrument.** The paper sheet and conditions slip each carry a version id, and
the participant-facing build is pinned as before. A change after P6.3 has run to the sheet, the slip, the
transcription or wizard screens, enrollment, the holdout gate or the reveal voids the rehearsal for the changed
parts and requires a re-run before F1, on the same rule as above for any participant-instrument change. So the
facilitated-format work items listed in
[PROJECT-PLAN.md section 11a](../PROJECT-PLAN.md#f1-facilitated-format-work-items-decided-2026-11-24) must be merged
before P6.3 runs. As of 2026-11-25 the session-scoped wizard (#216) and the transcription timing work (#218) are
merged; the group blotter code pull request (#220) and the group safeguards pull request (#221) are merged too but
group blotter mode is not used by F1 (see the plan). Sheet version `facilitated-session-sheet v2.2` is the version
P6.3 must rehearse. The v2.2 change adds the Atomizer row under the blind code row on each evaluator header; the six
rehearsal headers must be reprinted with the v2.2 layout before the 3.2 sittings.

**Deliberate failures in the P6.3 dry run.** These are the same four cases the facilitated-format scope lists, with the
individual-mode behavior verified in code (#226):

1. A sheet entered under the wrong evaluator is expected to be refused by the blind-code confirmation
   (`confirm_blind_code`, 409).
2. Withdrawal of one of the six documents the known gap: there is no withdraw route, and the household reveal waits
   (`DisclosureGate.household_blocker`).
3. A holdout observation whose times pre-date the evaluator's own checkpoint is expected to be refused (422).
4. A holdout write for an evaluator who has no checkpoint of their own is expected to be refused (409), because the
   gate is per enrollment in an individual program.

**Not scaled (kept exactly).** The profile does not relax any of these:

- the `PROJECT-PLAN.md` invariants around the authenticated proxy, including no direct backend path that bypasses the

- the blind-to-identity mapping stays private;
- the paper is kept;
- the pre-exposure values are written down before the first exposure (see the [F1 gate](f1.md));
- a change to the participant build, to the session sheet or to the conditions slip after the P6.3 rehearsal voids
  that rehearsal, as stated above;
- the property guide stays outside the P6.3 instrument.

Nothing in this profile is evidence. Each retained record named in the table at the top of this page is still owed at

`docker-host` returned 502s, then went fully offline after a partial fix. Three chained defects
