**Status:** Planned; blocked on P6

**Owners:** Product owner and core maintainer

F1 is the first use of actual pilot perfumes. It validates product usability and establishes
descriptive baselines; it is not evidence that a recommendation algorithm is accurate.

Before the first exposure, record the pilot dates, evaluators, exact physical samples, algorithm
and prompt versions, candidate strategy, source snapshot, exclusions, support procedure, and
stopping conditions in a private evidence location. Also name, in that declaration, the person who
retrieves the blind-to-identity mapping and labels physical samples, and confirm whether that
person is also one of the four evaluators (a manager who is also an evaluator cannot be made blind
by software; see the note below).

| Evidence | Required contents |
| :--- | :--- |
| Use and support log | UI-only task completion, abandonment, errors, help required, connectivity failures, and recoveries |
| Measurement export | Eligible impressions, explicit responses, interest, sampling, liking, wear/buy, unavailability, coverage, variety, and every denominator |
| Provenance record | Population, window, versions, strategy, filters, exclusions, source snapshot, and export hash |
| Blind-baseline instrument completeness | Participant-instrument version (R7b build) recorded on the activated `Program`; per-field non-null counts across all four evaluators' pre-reveal rows, with denominators, for every first-blind-moment field named in `preference-prediction-data-capture-assessment.md` section 13.13 and `PROJECT-PLAN.md`'s R7a/R7b rows; count of deviation/quality flags; count of consent records |
| Pilot review | Usability findings separated from fragrance and algorithm outcomes; small-sample limits stated |
| Decision | Record proceed to D1, revise a P3-P6 workflow, extend the declared pilot, or stop |

The evaluator who also holds the identity mapping is not blind for the duration of F1. That
evaluator's rows carry a quality flag marking them non-blind and are excluded from blind-baseline
training; the other evaluators' data is unaffected. This is a structural limitation, not a defect
to fix in software, and must be recorded in the pre-exposure declaration rather than discovered
afterward.

P6 evidence must remain valid throughout F1. A material deployment, disclosure, identity, or
measurement change pauses the pilot until the affected readiness checks are repeated.

Only `proceed to D1` completes F1 for sequencing. A revise, extend, or stop decision leaves F1
open and keeps D1 blocked until a later reviewed pilot records `proceed to D1`.
