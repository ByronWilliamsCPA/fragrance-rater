Use this guide only after a manager says the current rehearsal or pilot is ready. During P6,
use samples and outcomes clearly labeled as synthetic or demo data. Actual pilot perfume testing
starts only after the P6 readiness record says `go`.

## Sign in and choose your profile

1. Open the Fragrance Rater address supplied by the manager.
2. Complete the Authentik sign-in screen. Do not share your session or use another person's
   account.
3. Confirm that the name shown in the application is the evaluator you intend to record.
4. Stop and contact the manager if the wrong evaluator, unexpected manager navigation, or another
   person's history appears.

## Record an ordinary rating

Open **My Ratings**, choose the exact fragrance version, and record the encounter. A later
encounter creates another history entry; it does not erase an earlier experience. Leave a field
blank when it was not assessed. A zero is a real response.

## Complete a blind calibration session

Open **Your calibration** and choose the assigned program. Work from the blind code on the
physical label. Before reveal, the application should not show the fragrance identity, role,
repeat relationship, holdout status, or manager mapping.

1. Smell the blotter and record only what you perceive.
2. Submit the blotter response, then lock that stage when it is complete.
3. Apply only samples included in the manager-approved skin plan.
4. Record elapsed skin observations at the actual time observed and lock the stage when finished.
5. Ask the recorder to finalize the skin plan, including an intentionally empty plan.
6. Reveal identities only when the application says every prerequisite is complete.
7. Add any post-reveal response in the separate post-reveal area.

Do not infer identities from bottle order, labels, browser history, or another participant. Do not
photograph or share the manager's mapping sheet before reveal.

## Review recommendations

Open **Recommendations** to create a new set or reopen a saved set. Record interest before
sampling. If you later sample a recommendation, update its sampling state and link the later
ordinary outcome when available. An explanation can help describe the deterministic match, but
it is not a probability or a promise that you will like the fragrance.

## Interruption or connectivity failure

After reconnecting, reopen the same page and verify the last saved response before submitting
again. If the application cannot be reached, use the manager's
[printed evaluation and outage form](../printable-evaluation-form.md). Record the blind code,
stage, elapsed minutes, and local time. Give the form to the assigned recorder; do not write the
concealed identity on it. The recorder will enter the data and record the operational failure
after service returns.

## Report a problem

Stop the session and contact the manager if:

- an identity or experimental role appears before reveal;
- a saved response disappears or changes;
- the application allows entry after a stage is locked;
- the wrong evaluator or program is shown;
- the page cannot be completed with the keyboard or on the assigned device;
- a severe physical reaction occurs: stop exposure immediately, seek emergency help under the
  local emergency procedure, and notify the manager once it is safe to do so.

The application records preferences, observations, and operational events. Avoid entering
passwords, medical details, or unrelated personal information in free-text fields.
