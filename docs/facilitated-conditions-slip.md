Slip version: `facilitated-conditions-slip v1.0`

Print one slip per evaluator per sitting, and only for an evaluator who has accepted the session-context consent in
the application (scope `session_context`). The approved consent text promises that these answers "are kept apart from
your ratings", so this slip is never part of the score sheet and never travels with it.

## Handling rules for the facilitator

1. Give the slip to the evaluator before any blotter is sprayed. The evaluator completes it alone and hands it back
   face down. Do not read the answers aloud or comment on them.
2. Keep slips in their own folder or envelope, separate from the score sheets. Never staple, clip, or file a slip with
   the sheet it belongs to. The shared **Paper record ID** is the only link between the two.
3. Enter the answers into the application's session-context form for that evaluator's session (the form on the
   session introduction step). The fields below are the same fields, in the same wording, so each row maps to one
   field. Leave a field empty when the slip row is empty (not asked); choose **Prefer not to answer** when the
   evaluator ticked it. Those two are different things in the data.
4. Do not enter anything for an evaluator who has not accepted the session-context consent, and do not hand them a
   slip. Taking part never depends on answering.
5. Do not make a decision about the evaluator's data from these answers (for example, dropping a sitting because the
   evaluator was congested). They are recorded for later sensitivity analysis only.
6. After entry, tick the **Entered** box and keep the slip under the same retention rule as the score sheets (see the
   [facilitator run sheet](facilitated-run-sheet.md#paper-retention-and-scans)).
7. The sheet version on the score sheet and the slip version above are both named in the F1 pre-exposure declaration.
   A change to a question, a choice, or the wording of this slip is a new slip version.
