## Property guide: the eight "What you perceived" rows

facilitated-property-guide v1.1

**How to use this page.** You get this page once, at the start, and keep it for every session. Keep it beside your
sheet. It explains, in plain words, the eight rows under **What you perceived**. These rows ask what the smell **is**, not whether you like it. Liking and discomfort are scored
separately, under **How you responded**. There are no right answers, and 0 is a real answer. Use only your own nose:
do not compare with anyone, and do not discuss the samples until the household reveal.

If a row still makes no sense after reading this page, leave it blank. That is fine. If it makes sense but you do not
notice it in the smell, circle 0 (for Intensity, 0 means you cannot smell the sample). Otherwise pick the number that
feels nearest. A first impression is enough. Numbers 1 to 4 sit between the two ends.

| Row on your sheet | What to notice |
| --- | --- |
| **Intensity**<br>0 not detected, 5 overpowering | How strong the smell is, like the volume on a speaker. This is about strength only. If you cannot smell the sample at all, circle 0, write the times, initial the column, and leave the rest of that column blank. |
| **Sweetness**<br>0 not sweet, 5 very sweet | How sweet the smell seems, the way a drink can taste more or less sugary. |
| **Freshness**<br>0 not fresh, 5 very fresh | How crisp, cool and bright the smell feels, like cool air on your face. A low number does not mean bad. It means the smell does not feel crisp or cool. This is separate from Clean or soapy. |
| **Density**<br>0 thin and airy, 5 thick and heavy | How much the smell seems to fill the space around it, whatever its strength. This is not the same as Intensity: a faint smell can feel thick, and a strong one can feel thin. Think of a thin mist next to a thick fog. |
| **Dryness**<br>0 not dry, 5 very dry | How dry the smell feels, as opposed to moist or steamy. Dry is not the opposite of sweet. A smell can be both. |
| **Clean or soapy**<br>0 not at all, 5 strongly | The smell of soap, clean laundry or a scrubbed sink. This is about that soapy smell, not about how cool or crisp it feels. |
| **Earthy or rooty**<br>0 not at all, 5 strongly | Like soil, roots or a forest floor: dark and natural, from the ground. |
| **Bodily or animalic**<br>0 not at all, 5 strongly | Like hair, breath or a living animal up close: from a living body. |

No row is good or bad. Some people enjoy a smell and some do not. You only say how much you notice.
