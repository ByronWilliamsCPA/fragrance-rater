## Property guide: the eight "What you perceived" rows

**How to use this page.** Keep it beside your sheet. It explains, in plain words, the eight rows under **What you
perceived**. These rows ask what the smell **is**, not whether you like it. Liking and discomfort are scored
separately, under **How you responded**. There are no right answers, and 0 is a real answer. Use only your own nose:
do not compare with anyone, and do not discuss the samples until the household reveal.

If a word means nothing to you, leave that row blank. Do not guess. Close is good enough: pick the number that fits
best. Numbers 1 to 4 sit between the two ends.

| Row on your sheet | What to notice |
| --- | --- |
| **Intensity**<br>0 nothing at all, 5 overpowering | How strong the smell is, like the volume on a speaker. This is about strength only. |
| **Sweetness**<br>0 not sweet, 5 very sweet | How sweet the smell seems, the way a drink can taste more or less sugary. |
| **Freshness**<br>0 not fresh, 5 very fresh | How crisp, cool and bright the smell feels, like cool air on your face. A low number does not mean bad. It means the smell does not feel crisp or cool. |
| **Density**<br>0 thin and airy, 5 thick and heavy | How much the smell seems to fill the space around it, whatever its strength. Thin and airy is like a light scarf. Thick and heavy is like a wool blanket. |
| **Dryness**<br>0 not dry, 5 very dry | How dry the smell feels, as opposed to moist or juicy. Think of dry paper next to a damp sponge. |
| **Clean or soapy**<br>0 not at all, 5 strongly | A freshly washed, scrubbed quality, as if something has just been rinsed. Rate how strongly you notice that quality. |
| **Earthy or rooty**<br>0 not at all, 5 strongly | A damp, dark, natural quality that feels low and close to the ground. It describes the smell. It is not a good mark or a bad mark. |
| **Bodily or animalic**<br>0 not at all, 5 strongly | A warm, raw, close-up quality, like the warmth of a living body. It describes the smell. It is not a good mark or a bad mark. |

Some people enjoy a quality and some do not. Either way, you only say how much of it you notice.
